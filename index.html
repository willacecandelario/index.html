BusTrack PH

COMMUT3RS

BusTrackPH is a real-time bus monitoring system designed to improve the daily commuting experience. It allows passengers to track bus locations, check estimated arrival times, view passenger occupancy, and receive priority seat alerts, making public transportation more convenient, efficient, and reliable.

Business Background

BusTrackPH became our idea because we noticed and experienced how frustrating it is waiting for uncertainty when it comes to buses especially in areas such as Sto. Thomas to Davao, and vice versa.

Vision

We seek to improve the overall commuting experience of people from the Municipality of Sto. Tomas and citizens of Carmen.

Mission

We aim to transform the commuting experience of people in the Municipality of Sto. Tomas and the Municipality of Carmen by providing smarter, safer, and more convenient public transportation through BusTrackPH.

Background Information

Waiting for something that is uncertain is behind the reason why BusTrackPH became an idea!

The Problem

Commuters often wait for buses without knowing when they will arrive and how full the vehicles are, especially on routes such as Sto. Tomas to Davao and vice versa.

Our Solution

BusTrackPH is a real-time bus monitoring system that shows bus locations, estimated arrival times, and passenger occupancy in one place.

Who It Is For

Daily commuters and passengers in the Municipality of Sto. Tomas and the Municipality of Carmen, including those who need priority seating.

Key Features

Live bus tracking, estimated arrival times, passenger occupancy updates, and priority seat alerts.
